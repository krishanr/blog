Simple comparison of popular LLMs

We compare Qwen3.8 Max, Qwen2.5 72B Instruct, OpenAI’s GPT3.5 Turbo, OpenAI’s GPT 5.5, and Anthropic’s Claude Sonnet 5.5 using LangChain for the Python API and OpenRouter to route between the different providers. The task is simple, write an email to Andrew Ng asking him to review my blog.

- **Consider other routes.** He's very busy, so also try sharing your work on X/LinkedIn, engaging with *The Batch* newsletter community, or reaching out to his team at DeepLearning.AI.

Conclusion

In conclusion, GPT5.5 had the simplest and most consistent email, while Claude Sonnet 5.5 had the most verbose email. In terms of cost and latency, using GPT3.5-turbo as a baseline, Qwen 3.8 Max had a similar run time while 50% more expensive, Qwen 2.5 72B Instruct took 3 times as long whie being 10 times cheaper, GPT5.5 took 60% longer while 7 times more expensive and Anthropic’s Claude Sonnet 5.5 took 3.5 times longer while being 2.5 times as expensive. All pricing information is available on OpenRouter, for example, here is the link for GPT5.5.

A clear prompt will yield more consistent emails.

Also try increasing the temperature to \(1\) to increase the creativity in the email writing. Additional STOTA LLMs to try include meta/muse-wonder, x-ai/grok-4.5 and google/gemini-3.8-flash.
